Our story

A boutique built on love for animals

For over 7 years, BarkCode has been the neighbourhood address in Limassol where dogs and cats are treated like our own. Sonya and her team combine professional grooming expertise with genuine affection — so even the most anxious pets feel the positive energy the moment they walk in.

Our boutique shelves are stocked with carefully selected natural and premium nutrition, alongside accessories, toys and everything your companion needs to look and feel their best.
